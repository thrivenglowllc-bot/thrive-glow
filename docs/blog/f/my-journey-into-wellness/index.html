My journey into Wellness...

July 6, 2026 · Theresa

If you had told me years ago that I would become an Integrative Health Practitioner and spend my days helping women improve their hormones, gut health, energy, and overall well-being, I probably wouldn't have believed you.

Like many women, my health journey began because I was searching for answers.

For years, I struggled with symptoms that I thought were normal. In 2017, I began experiencing severe stomach pain almost every night. The pain became so common that I found myself relying on ibuprofen just to get through it. I felt frustrated, uncomfortable, and unsure of what was causing my symptoms.

At the time, I didn't realize that my body was trying to communicate with me.

After a lot of trial and error, I discovered that dairy was a major trigger for me. Within just one week of removing dairy from my diet, I lost five pounds of inflammation and began noticing significant improvements in how I felt. While that was a huge breakthrough, it was only the beginning of my health journey.

A few years later, I decided to stop taking hormonal birth control. That's when a new set of challenges appeared. My periods became extremely painful, lasting anywhere from seven to ten days. Every month, I found myself reaching for ibuprofen to manage the cramps and discomfort. Again, I was told that painful periods were simply part of being a woman.

But deep down, I knew there had to be more to the story.

That's when I discovered the world of functional and integrative health.

For the first time, I learned that symptoms are not random. They are clues. Instead of asking, "How do I get rid of this symptom?" I started asking, "Why is this happening in the first place?"

I began learning about hormones, gut health, inflammation, nutrition, stress, sleep, and how all of these systems work together. I implemented lifestyle changes, improved my nutrition, and started cycle syncing—aligning my food, exercise, and lifestyle habits with the different phases of my menstrual cycle.

The results were incredible.

Over time, my periods became shorter, my cramps dramatically improved, my energy increased, and I experienced less inflammation overall. I felt more connected to my body than ever before. What once felt like a constant battle became a partnership.

As my own health improved, something unexpected happened. I became passionate about helping others experience the same transformation.

I realized that so many women are walking around believing that exhaustion, bloating, painful periods, mood swings, stubborn weight gain, and digestive issues are simply things they have to live with. I know what it feels like to be frustrated, confused, and searching for answers.

That experience inspired me to become an Integrative Health Practitioner.

Today, I help women uncover the root causes behind their symptoms through personalized coaching, functional lab testing, nutrition, and lifestyle strategies. My goal is not to chase symptoms but to help women understand what their bodies are trying to tell them.

I believe our bodies were designed to heal when given the right support. While there is no one-size-fits-all approach to health, there is always value in listening to the signals our bodies provide.

My journey taught me that healing is possible, even when it feels far away. It taught me that symptoms are messengers, not enemies. Most importantly, it taught me that true health isn't about perfection—it's about creating habits that help you thrive.

If you're struggling with symptoms and feel like you've run out of answers, know that you're not alone. I've been there too.

And sometimes, your greatest challenge becomes the very thing that equips you to help someone else.

Thank you for being here and allowing me to be a part of your wellness journey.

With gratitude,

Theresa
Founder, Thrive & Glow Wellness
