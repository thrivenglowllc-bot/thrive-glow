What do you know about your Liver?

September 2, 2025 · {{owner_name}}

Did you know your liver is one of the hardest-working organs in the body? It is associated with over 500 vital bodily functions, winning the award as the body's ultimate multitasker!

Your liver is a powerful detoxification organ that acts as a filter, removing toxins from your blood to be excreted through the digestive system. It also...

  • Produces bile, a fluid essential for digestion and keeping bad bugs at bay
  • Stores and releases glucose when your body needs quick energy
  • It helps maintain blood sugar levels
  • Metabolizes nutrients, vitamins, and minerals

The liver does a LOT of things, and it's important we support it so it can do all its jobs optimally and improve the overall health functioning of every cell and organ in the body!

But wait- your liver does more!

  • It regenerates itself- your liver is the only solid organ in the body that regenerates. Even if up to 70% of it is removed or damaged, it can grow back to its full size in just a few months.
  • Beyond detoxing- it regulates hormones, breaks down fats, and even helps with immune defense.
  • It stores emotions- in Traditional Chinese Medicine, the liver is linked to anger, frustration, and stress. When your liver is sluggish, it's common to feel more irritable or moody.
  • Your skin is a reflection of liver health- acne, rashes, rosacea, and even hyperpigmentation can be signs that your liver is overloaded or struggling to clear out toxins efficiently.

Your liver is powerful, but it's constantly under an enormous amount of stress, and without living a detoxifying lifestyle, you're adding more and more stress to it every single day. Our modern world is FULL of toxins; our body is full of things creating toxins, and the more toxins your liver has to process, the more stress it's under.

Your goal is to reduce the toxin burden on your body daily by lessening the toxins you're exposed to AND by lessening your toxins already in your body.

When toxins constantly bombard your liver, and you can't remove them as fast as they are added, it starts to back up. When that happens, it pushes those toxins back into your bloodstream, which are then deposited throughout your body, causing cell damage, stealing energy, and causing a whole host of symptoms. This is the LAST thing youwant!

Your liver detoxes in two phases. Phase 1- transforms toxins into intermediate forms, and Phase 2- neutralizes them so they can be eliminated.

Think of Phase 1 as the "sorting room". The liver takes fat-soluble toxins (from food, alcohol, medications, hormones, pesticides, pollution, etc) and chemically transforms them using enzymes.

The toxins are broken down into intermediate compounds- BUT here's the catch: these intermediates can actually be more harmful and reactive than the original toxin (like half-burnt wood releasing more smoke).

This is why Phase 2 must kick in quickly.

Phase 2 is the "packaging & shipping" phase. The liver attaches molecules to the toxic intermediates so they become water-soluble and safe to excrete. This process is called conjugation and has several pathways:

  • Glutathione conjugation- binds toxins to glutathione (master antioxidant)
  • Sulfation- uses sulfur compounds (from foods like garlic, onions, cruciferous veggies)
  • Glucuronidation- attaches glucuronic acid (helpful for estrogen detox)
  • Methylation- adds a methyl group (key for detoxing histamine + hormones)
  • Amino Acids Conjugation- uses amino acids like glycine and taurine

Once neutralized, toxins exit through bile, urine, or stool.
