How I Finally Figured Out What My Body Was Trying to Tell Me

September 25, 2025 · {{owner_name}}

In 2017, my health completely flipped upside down.

I was in so much stomach pain I felt like I couldn’t eat anything without regretting it. On top of that, I was gaining weight rapidly — and no matter what I tried, nothing worked. I went from doctor to doctor, hoping someone could tell me what was wrong. Instead, I walked away with no answers, only frustration.

That’s when I decided to take matters into my own hands.

I started paying attention to what I was eating, journaling my meals, and tracking how I felt. Slowly, I began to notice patterns — certain foods were triggering my pain and bloating. Dairy, for one, was definitely not my friend.

It was a wake-up call.

I started learning about nutrition and really looking at the ingredients in my food. I realized my diet was full of inflammatory foods I didn’t even know were harming me. So, I made changes.

And guess what? The stomach pain I had been living with completely disappeared. Not only that, but I lost weight and actually felt good in my body again.

But that was just the beginning of my journey.

Fast forward to 2020. After being on birth control for years, I decided it was time to stop. What I didn’t expect was how much my body would struggle. My periods turned into my worst nightmare — painful cramps that left me unable to move, heavy bleeding that lasted up to 10 days, and a monthly routine of popping ibuprofen just to survive.

It felt like I had traded one problem for another.

One day, I came across someone discussing Cycle Syncing. I was curious, so I started researching. I learned that by supporting my hormones in different phases of my cycle, I could actually help my body instead of fighting it.

And it worked.

Now, my periods are completely different — little to no pain, just five days long, and with a normal flow. I no longer dread that time of the month. Instead, I feel in control of my body.

Because of all my experience, I wanted to dig deeper and learn more about our bodies. I learned about vitamins, minerals, and how our hormones work, which introduced me to the world of Integrative Health. From there, I was able to continue to heal my body by getting Functional lab testing. That opened my eyes to a whole new world! I was able to get to the root causes of my bloating, gut, and hormones.

That transformation lit a fire in me. I realized that what I went through is something so many women struggle with — bloating, painful periods, fatigue, stubborn weight — and yet we’re often left without answers.

That’s why I became a certified 1 & 2 Integrative Health Practitioner. Today, I help women:

  • Balance hormones naturally
  • Beat the bloat that makes you feel like you “can’t eat anything”
  • Finally, have periods that don’t rule your life
  • Feel energized, confident, and in control again

Because I know what it’s like to feel defeated by your own body. And I also know what it’s like to finally feel free.
