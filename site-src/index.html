Sound familiar?

  • Bloating that builds through the day
  • Constipation, loose stools, or both
  • Weight that won’t move, mostly around the middle
  • Hot flashes or night sweats
  • Periods that changed without warning
  • Waking at night and not getting back to sleep
  • Afternoon crashes and sugar cravings
  • Mood swings or anxiety that feel new

Gut and hormones are tightly linked: digestion affects how your body clears estrogen, and hormone shifts change digestion. Looking at both together is usually where the answers are.
